LII-193: Enamel Box

A Fortune Hunter / A Buxom Widow

A light-blue oval enamel box with a hinged lid. The box is made of metal and enamel. On top of the lid, the figures of a man and a woman are rendered in white, both wearing hats on their heads. One end of the lid is inscribed “A Fortune Hunter” and the other end “A Buxom Widow”. Floral motifs and rose decorations run all around the box. This box is from England and is believed to date to the 19th century CE.
