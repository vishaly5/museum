7. Peacock (K.K. Hebbar)

Acc No: ACQ-66-71

This painting depicts a peacock, signed by the artist in the lower left corner. The peacock is a recurring theme in Hebbar's art, expressing his attraction to rhythm, motion, and joy of life. Part of his 'Moods of Peacock' series (1968), it portrays India's national bird dancing with outstretched feathers.

Rather than strict realism, Hebbar abstracted the peacock's form into essential rhythmic elements. Vibrant color combinations and dynamic brushlines infuse energy into the composition, conveying the music and movement inherent in the subject.
